# Contributing

Only submit independently authored, general-purpose, redistributable survey and CAD methods.

Do not submit employer-specific procedures, client documents, actual registered-plan scans, private coordinates, proprietary blocks, credentials, job identifiers, or third-party copyrighted text without permission.

Use synthetic test fixtures with expected numeric outcomes. Record source authority, bearing basis, units, feature/endpoint identities and uncertainty. Distinguish conceptual guidance from independently tested algorithms. All contributions require a confidentiality and attribution review.
