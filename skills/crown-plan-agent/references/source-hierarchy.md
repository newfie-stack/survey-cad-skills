# Public regulatory source hierarchy

## Source inventory inspected
- Government of Alberta, **Public Lands Survey Manual**, version 2.3, May 31, 2024. Its page 5 explicitly supersedes version 2.2, October 20, 2022 in entirety; the publisher marks v2.2 out of date. It describes jurisdiction, application stages, plan/sketch types, hybrid and compiled surveys, and disposition-specific considerations while preserving primary regulator documentation and professional obligations.
- Government of Alberta, **Content Requirements for Survey Plans and Sketches for Disposition and Disposition Reservation Activities Affecting Public Lands**, version 4.0, September 2022. Historic general/sketch/survey/activity requirements. Do not treat as current when a newer applicable publication supersedes it.
- Government of Alberta, **Content Requirements for Survey Plans and Sketches for Dispositions on Crown Lands**, 30 May 2024. Part 1 general requirements; Part 2 sketches and surveyor sketches; Part 3 survey plans (including amendments, hybrid cadastre and compiled plans); Part 4 activity-specific requirements. The document states the Open Government Licence – Alberta.
- Additional reference inventory includes Public Lands Act, Public Lands Administration Regulation, PLAR tables, GIS quality/framework publications, plan submission guidance, and regulator-specific instructions. Each requires current-version and applicability checks.

## Authority resolution
Consult the [November 21, 2026 source audit](regulatory-source-audit-2026-11-21.md)
for linked editions, explicit supersession, inspected clauses and remaining gaps.
It also verifies the June 2026 A1, October 2026 A2 and effective April 2026 MSP editions.
The earlier [November 18 observations](regulatory-source-audit-2026-11-18.md) remain historical.
A dated cover does not
prove supersession, and a current publisher listing does not prove current
clause applicability. Keep catalogue cutoff, consolidation, publication, content
inspection and effective dates separate.

1. Identify jurisdiction, activity, plan type and regulator before selecting requirements.
2. Consult current official legislation and regulation, then current regulator guidance for the applicable activity and stage.
3. Record title, publication date/version, clause, official URL, access date, and whether a newer publication replaces it.
4. Use archived documents only to interpret legacy plans, with HISTORICAL_SOURCE status.
5. Never use a private example or office preference as regulatory authority.
6. If official sources conflict, report CONFLICT_REVIEW rather than guessing priority.

Official discovery entry points:
- https://open.alberta.ca/
- https://www.alberta.ca/
- https://www.aer.ca/

Do not copy entire manuals into a public skill repository. Prefer short original summaries and links to the regulator's public copy.
