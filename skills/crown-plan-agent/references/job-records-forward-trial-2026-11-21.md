# Fresh job-record workflow trial — November 21, 2026

This trial evaluates assisted preparation and execution on six freshly invented
job bundles, with two clean controls and four authored problem cases. An author
prepared original plan/application/registry and supporting-record PDFs plus job
briefs without reading the implementation. The oracle stays hidden until the
review outputs are frozen. No work document supplies inputs or expected answers.

## Frozen procedure

Freeze the engine, skill and referenced review instructions before reviewing.
Record every source-file hash and input-manifest/oracle hash. Reviewers receive
only their opaque bundle directories, the actual job briefs and frozen skill.
They inspect actual original page images, read the complete titleblock/Notes and
reference records, then prepare private source-bound selections and run supported
job comparisons/balances. No selectors, answers or defect count are supplied.

Each reviewer preserves their literal explanation, specification, actual CLI/API
records and requested PDF review copy. Unsupported evidence stays unresolved.
Freeze those outputs before independent grading. Grade source-supported issues,
unresolved obligations, false alarms, false clearances and duplicate claims
separately; credit assisted observations separately from executed predicates.

Record compilation/full-suite/package outcomes independently of the model trial.
Once fixtures are published, they become known regressions rather than fresh
blind inputs. Source and evidence preservation may add tests/documentation after
the trial without changing the frozen implementation used for those reviews.

This is procedural blinding on a shared filesystem, not a security boundary or
an estimate of accuracy on all real plans. The outcome does not establish
current application-specific authority, accepted field geometry or qualified
professional approval. Exact results and limitations are recorded after grading.

## Initial six-job results

The engine and review instructions were frozen at commit
`a4c25a1db7c7da83cb73ffddd4b768f6ac13360f` before preparation. All reviews were
frozen before independent graders received the static oracle. The two clean
controls and four problem jobs used 27 original invented input files.

| False alarms / false clearances / unsupported claims / duplicate credit | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 |
| Requested / executed / blocked comparisons | 88 / 21 / 67 |
| Executed PASS / FAIL / unresolved | 20 / 1 / 0 |

The missing current approved baseline was preserved as an assisted unresolved
dependency, not a drawing defect or executed detection. One job needed a second
preparation attempt; both attempts and the final selections are retained. These
results do not mean four automatic detections or unattended preparation.

The trial exposed excessive continuation blocking in dense records. Independent
actual-PDF probes then corrected recognition of complete typed labelled fields
and quantities while preserving ambiguous labels, continuations and units.
The original results were not overwritten.

## Known-preparation replay

The same original saved preparation bytes were replayed after the correction at
commit `b5624579add28116bd2e0152781140a763249e01`. All per-finding statuses stayed
the same: 88 requests, 21 executed (20 PASS, 1 FAIL), 67 blocked. This is a known
compatibility regression, not a new blind trial or evidence of better accuracy
on the original layouts.

## Two new holdouts

Two further bundles, containing eight original input files, were authored
separately and reviewed under the corrected frozen implementation. Reviewers
received no oracle or prepared selectors. Outputs were frozen before grading.

Both bundles had **no planted drawing defects**, so no detection recall can be
calculated. Ten area comparisons executed and passed; twelve identity requests
were blocked. Eleven blocks were unnecessary abstentions on source-resolvable
identity fields; one represented a registry cell with two possible governing
legal names and no effective-date basis. Assisted reading correctly retained
that dependency without selecting a name. There were no false alarms, false
clearances, unsupported findings or duplicate credit in this set. A generic
engine block does not earn detection credit for the assisted finding.

The dense-field change therefore has bounded regression evidence, not a claim
that identity intake is solved. Further work needs genuinely new inputs and
separate reporting of preparation, predicate execution and assisted findings.

## Preservation and reproducibility

`tests/job_record_forward_examples.py` recreates all eight invented bundles.
`tests/fixtures/job_record_forward_truth.json` preserves original preparation,
execution, grading and later replay evidence separately. Twelve tests in
`tests/test_job_record_forward_examples.py` verify byte-identical regeneration
of all 35 inputs in the tested runtime, immutable truth, actual source-bound
execution, replay results and source-change rejection. No model is called by
these regression tests. Published cases are now known inputs, not fresh blind
holdouts.

The original 92-file source/instruction snapshot is preserved with the raw trial
artifacts outside the runtime package. Procedural blinding used a shared
filesystem; it was not a security boundary. Exact underlying reviewer model
metadata was not recorded. The small invented sets establish neither general
accuracy nor learning over time.
