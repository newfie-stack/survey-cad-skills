# Fresh job-record workflow trial — November 21, 2026

This trial evaluates assisted preparation and execution on six freshly invented
job bundles, with two clean controls and four authored problem cases. An author
prepared original plan/application/registry and supporting-record PDFs plus job
briefs without reading the implementation. The oracle stays hidden until the
review outputs are frozen. No work document supplies inputs or expected answers.

## Frozen procedure

Freeze the engine, skill and referenced review instructions before reviewing.
Record every source-file hash and input-manifest/oracle hash. Reviewers receive
only their opaque bundle directories, the actual job briefs and frozen skill.
They inspect actual original page images, read the complete titleblock/Notes and
reference records, then prepare private source-bound selections and run supported
job comparisons/balances. No selectors, answers or defect count are supplied.

Each reviewer preserves their literal explanation, specification, actual CLI/API
records and requested PDF review copy. Unsupported evidence stays unresolved.
Freeze those outputs before independent grading. Grade source-supported issues,
unresolved obligations, false alarms, false clearances and duplicate claims
separately; credit assisted observations separately from executed predicates.

Record compilation/full-suite/package outcomes independently of the model trial.
Once fixtures are published, they become known regressions rather than fresh
blind inputs. Source and evidence preservation may add tests/documentation after
the trial without changing the frozen implementation used for those reviews.

This is procedural blinding on a shared filesystem, not a security boundary or
an estimate of accuracy on all real plans. The outcome does not establish
current application-specific authority, accepted field geometry or qualified
professional approval. Exact results and limitations are recorded after grading.
