# Interactive Artifact Review

Represent each reviewable artifact with a stable entity ID, viewable geometry or document representation, source references, derivation records, validation status and reviewer decisions. Make the review interface observational: rendering or selection must not silently alter source geometry or acceptance state. For vector objects, provide selection and inspection of geometry type, coordinates, attributes, source evidence and computed relationships. Clearly distinguish accepted, provisional, failed and unresolved entities. Keep stale asynchronous responses from replacing newer selections. Require an explicit authorized action for approval or mutation; preserve an audit trail and exportable findings. Use synthetic data in examples.
