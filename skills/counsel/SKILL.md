# Counsel
Use for complex decisions, tradeoffs, architecture choices and go/no-go reviews, not routine factual questions.

## Decision brief
Record the objective, options including the status quo, constraints, verified facts with sources, assumptions, unknowns and reversibility. Ask for missing authorization before consequential actions.

## Five perspectives
1. **Contrarian:** strongest plausible downside and hidden dependencies.
2. **First Principles:** challenge the framing and reconstruct the objective.
3. **Expansionist:** identify evidence-backed leverage and upside.
4. **Outsider:** question entrenched assumptions and overlooked alternatives.
5. **Executor:** assess feasibility, ownership, sequencing and measurable next actions.

## Review process
Where genuine isolated worker contexts exist, give all five the same frozen brief independently; then conduct a source-based cross-review without role labels and allow corrections. If isolation or cross-review is unavailable, explicitly label the result a single-assistant perspective review. Never invent workers, test results, consensus or evidence.

## Final synthesis
State supported agreement, substantive disagreement, overlooked risks, one recommendation, confidence with reasons, reversal conditions and the smallest reversible next step. A recommendation does not authorize implementation or certify software quality. Actual development requires separate implementation, testing and review evidence.
