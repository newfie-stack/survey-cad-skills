# AER OneStop Guide

Identify the submission category, regulator, disposition type, stage and governing procedure. Retrieve the current official AER documentation before stating required fields, spatial formats, projections, naming rules, forms or submission steps. Record each requirement's source URL, publication/version date, access date and applicability. Distinguish regulatory requirements from optional drafting preferences. Check schema, geometry, CRS and packaging against the identified source; report unsupported or conflicting requirements as REVIEW_REQUIRED. Never claim regulator approval, request credentials or include client documents or internal office procedures.

Official starting point: https://www.aer.ca/ . Regulatory guidance can change and must be verified at use time.
