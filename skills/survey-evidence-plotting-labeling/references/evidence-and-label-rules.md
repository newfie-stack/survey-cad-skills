# Evidence and label rules
- Retain raw monument descriptions and source citations; never translate abbreviations without the plan legend.
- A monument or witness marker may be offset from the legal corner. Preserve its observation, offset and role separately.
- Use source-specific found, placed, calculated, not-monumented and re-established descriptions.
- A countersunk marker and a below-grade vertical note are not interchangeable unless the source says so.
- Prefer project-supplied authorized CAD symbols/styles; do not reproduce employer-owned blocks or naming conventions.
- A dynamic block must preserve insertion point, attributes, rotation and scale after save/reopen.
- Verify no duplicate evidence, misplaced label, or unsupported legal-location description.
- For boundary-only exports, omit annotation and evidence when not requested, while retaining them in the audit record.
