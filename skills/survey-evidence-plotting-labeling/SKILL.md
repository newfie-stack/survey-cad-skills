# Survey Evidence Plotting & Labeling
Use after source interpretation and accepted COGO. Separate monument evidence, legal boundaries, control, annotations and review-only items. Preserve found/placed/calculated status, original evidence descriptions and the source plan's symbology. Place labels from supported coordinates and roles, never from image proximity alone. Validate scale, legibility, units, orientation, leader attachment and annotation duplication. Refer to [evidence and label rules](references/evidence-and-label-rules.md).
