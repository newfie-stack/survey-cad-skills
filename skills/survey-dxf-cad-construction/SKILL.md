# Survey DXF & CAD Construction
Use deterministic COGO for bearings, distances, offsets, intersections, curves and transformations. Keep survey/control, subject boundaries, roads, evidence and annotation distinct. Preserve surveyed XY coordinates; identify derived connectivity and local provisional geometry. When editing existing drawings, retain unrelated accepted entities and metadata. Use explicit CRS/units and target-CAD compatibility requirements. Reopen generated DXF and check entity counts, extents, layers, closure, widths, terminal intersections, duplicates, areas and residuals. A file opening successfully is not proof of survey correctness. Never silently overwrite accepted drawings with partial exports. Use synthetic examples only.
