# Quarter Completeness & Encumbrance Research
Use after reconstructing a parcel or quarter and before claiming a complete registered-interest background. Consult authoritative land titles and registered-plan indexes where legally accessible. Distinguish boundary-bearing registered plans from title interests and unregistered/contextual GIS data. Record sources, dates, status, plan identifiers and outstanding research; never assert that a quarter is clear solely because a GIS layer is empty. See [completeness protocol](references/completeness-protocol.md).
