# Completeness protocol
1. Establish the target quarter/parcel and effective search date.
2. Search authoritative registered plan indexes for affected boundaries and relevant dispositions.
3. Cross-check title instruments, easements, utility rights, restrictive covenants and other interests where applicable.
4. Distinguish an interest requiring geometry from an interest requiring only annotation or further legal review.
5. Reconcile duplicates, superseded plans, overlapping extents and uncertain locations.
6. Maintain a ledger: identifier, source, date, type, geometry status, title relevance, disposition, reviewer and open question.
7. Report COMPLETE only for the expressly scoped sources and date; otherwise PARTIAL or REVIEW_REQUIRED. Do not imply a title opinion or legal certification.
