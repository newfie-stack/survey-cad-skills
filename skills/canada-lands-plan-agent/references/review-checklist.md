# Canada Lands plan-review checklist

For each item record: **applicability**, actual evidence source/page/feature, source edition/section, `source_status`, `evidence_status` and PASS/FAIL/REVIEW_REQUIRED/NOT_APPLICABLE. These are review prompts; not every line is mandatory for every plan type. Avoid declaring a regulatory FAIL without current applicable authority.

## A. Jurisdiction / source verification
- [ ] Land class and jurisdiction verified: reserve, land code, other Canada Lands or provincial.
- [ ] First Nation/reserve name and number, land manager, province, IOGC involvement confirmed from records, not inferred.
- [ ] Transaction purpose, current surface contract type/number and holder, plus requested end-use confirmed.
- [ ] Applicable current legislation, National Standards, regional chapter, project SGB instructions and First Nation requirements checked.
- [ ] MyCLSS checklist and IOGC-specific requirements identified **only where applicable**.
- [ ] Document destination and type confirmed (as-built, Surface Permit sketch, Application Survey Plan, CLSR Survey Plan, compiled, amendment, partial surrender).

## B. Survey, land and interest inventory
- [ ] Entire title block, notes, area tables, diagram, detail sheets, legend read and reconciled.
- [ ] Recorded original CLSR numbers, provincial LTO numbers where relevant, associated agreement references distinguished (not merged).
- [ ] Earlier/historical plan geometry tagged by effective role; later plan does not automatically supersede title/rights.
- [ ] Existing lease/RW/access interests, current verified holders and reported status identified.
- [ ] Underlying road allowance shown separately from access tenure, jurisdiction confirmed and labelled.
- [ ] Known pipeline ROW crossing site/TWS shown even if its present legal status needs verification.
- [ ] Proposed new permanent rights, temporary incidental activities, and existing physical disturbance shown with unambiguous different extents.

## C. Application-specific drawing QC
- [ ] Document title and lands dealt with match application/instructions.
- [ ] Reserve/quarter/LSD/lot/parcel descriptions and section labels consistent where applicable.
- [ ] North arrow, main scale and details visually inspected; bearings, control and datum evidence examined.
- [ ] Dimensions, curves, ties, monumentation and parcel area calculations independently checked when source data available.
- [ ] Applicable signatures, CLSR/LTO recording blocks, endorsements, location and references correct for plan type.
- [ ] Existing relevant dispositions/authorizations and road allowance jurisdiction included.
- [ ] Required access route and other activity-specific tables, environmental/topographic content and wellbore descriptions checked **where applicable**.
- [ ] Source CAD/GIS and final plotted PDF consistent; do not claim digital validation without files and execution.

## D. IOGC incidental activities / remediation
- [ ] Proposed TWS and clearing extents individually labelled with activity, dimensions, impacted parcel(s) and area.
- [ ] For applicable IOGC Surface Lease/ROW formal survey plans, separate IA table in hectares, sub-totalled by activity type.
- [ ] IA area by parcel, physical total and overlap diagnostics reconcile; overlapping R/W counted **once** in total TWS.
- [ ] TWS is not depicted as permanent land dealt with unless explicitly supported by transaction.
- [ ] Formal survey-plan IA rule **not carried automatically** into Surface Permit sketch/as-built exhibit; verify exact deliverable requirement.
- [ ] Site status distinguished: active/suspended/abandoned, environmental remediation, reclamation, partial/full surrender.
- [ ] Off-lease work access route and First Nation/IOGC agreement or Surface Permit decision verified.
- [ ] Any substantial tree clearing escalated for land rights, site-specific environmental and timber permissions; no assumptions from proposed lines.

## E. Report quality
- [ ] Clear boundary-interest register and source citations.
- [ ] Clear list of unresolved land and agreement status instead of assumed active/cancelled rights.
- [ ] Mechanical checks (if any) distinguished from visual review and regulatory interpretation.
- [ ] Source check and plan check are two separate statuses; all missing facts remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
- [ ] No claims of CLSR recording, First Nation consent, IOGC approval, regulator acceptance or legal certification without actual proof.
- [ ] No identifiable case data, drawing or private coordinate saved to public repository.
