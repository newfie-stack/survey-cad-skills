# Decision matrix: tenure, survey and plan route

This is **triage**, not the regulator's decision. Obtain actual contract, First Nation approvals, current IOGC/NRCan instructions, and the intended application before selecting a final plan type.

| Case | Possible route | Mandatory open questions |
|---|---|---|
| Existing lease drawn as-built, boundaries unchanged | As-built/existing-condition exhibit; may accompany closure work | Is a new tenure transaction actually requested? Does the reviewer require a statutory plan? |
| TWS wholly outside an existing lease for short-term off-lease remediation | **Surface Permit (SP) candidate**; IOGC says SP requires a sketch using applicable provincial standards | Is the activity genuinely temporary? What permit or separate access rights are required? Does the First Nation approve? |
| Additional land permanently added to lease | Surface-contract amendment candidate; new survey/CLSR recording may be required if lands dealt with change | Exact amended contract, legal extent, CLS survey instructions, plan type |
| Change of use or additional infrastructure within existing lease | Possible amendment even without boundary change; some cases may not require new CLSR recording | Type of change, current contract, IOGC's specific direction |
| Partial surrender/retained remainder | Partial-surrender plan candidate | Survey required for retained and surrendered parcels, closure/reclamation status, contract/First Nation/SGB instructions |
| Abandonment, remediation or reclamation of existing site | Environmental/closure workflow, not by itself a boundary change | Is there an approved closure/remediation plan? Who administers the site? Will land or rights be surrendered? |
| Temporary workspace inside an existing pipeline ROW | New right/consent may still be needed | Are exclusive rights affected? Existing holder status; operation within authorized use |
| TWS crosses a pipeline ROW **outside** a separate site lease | Draw TWS distinctly; overlay existing ROW as an existing interest | Does IOGC/First Nation require additional consent or extra area breakdown? Track overlap, do not double count |
| Survey on First Nation reserve governed by Land Code, IOGC not verified | Land Code/First Nation requirements and SGB survey guidance | Does IOGC administer any relevant contract? What is the governing First Nation law? |
| Other Canada Lands (parks, northern territories, offshore) | Route to sector/jurisdiction-specific standards | Appropriate land administrator, region, plan type, statutes and survey instructions |
| Provincial Crown lands outside Canada Lands | **Out of scope** of this skill; use Crown Plan Agent | Alberta authority and appropriate current PLAR/other regime |

## Boundary-identity rules
1. **The plan is not the contract.** A CLSR plan depicts surveyed limits; legal rights and their present status must be independently checked against the applicable agreements and registry.
2. **Multiple record identifiers can be valid.** Same underlying plan may be referenced by its CLSR and provincial LTO numbers. Preserve both when applicable; never invent equivalence.
3. **Older does not necessarily mean cancelled.** An older pipeline ROW located inside a later lease drawing stays an existing-interest candidate until its legal status is resolved.
4. **Access and road allowance may overlap.** Label both the underlying road allowance (after jurisdiction check) and the access tenure/plan. Use separate geometry/roles even if their lines coincide.
5. **Existing disturbance is not a lease boundary.** Historic cleared area and remaining infrastructure should be shown/labelled as physical conditions, not a substitute for surveyed tenure.
6. **Incidental vs permanent.** TWS and log decks are normally temporary activities and should remain outside permanent lands dealt with unless the documented transaction indicates otherwise.
7. **Area attribution.** IA tables required by the applicable formal IOGC checklist are independent of overlap diagnostics. A pipeline ROW overlay does not require invented Alberta-style split rows.
8. **Tree clearing is separate.** No site plan, TWS polygon or area calculation establishes authority to remove timber; determine the actual approvals.

## Escalation trigger
If the exact transaction, recorded agreement status, land governance, source standard edition, or project-specific instruction is not established, present 1-3 likely alternatives with what evidence would choose among them. Set the specific normative finding to `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and never imply an application was approved.
