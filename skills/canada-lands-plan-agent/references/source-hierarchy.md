# Canada Lands sources and hierarchy

**Last inspected:** 2026-11-21. Always recheck the publisher for the current edition and amendments before issuing a normative plan finding. The URLs below are official source discovery points, not embedded copies of authoritative text.

1. **Applicable Acts and regulations:** Canada Lands Surveys Act; Indian Oil and Gas Act and Indian Oil and Gas Regulations, 2019 (where IOGC applies); Indian Act; land management framework and other applicable statutes. The exact land governance regime must be determined first.
   - https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-6/
   - https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-196/
2. **Surveyor General technical requirements:** current National Standards for the Survey of Canada Lands, current regional chapters/addenda, and **project-specific SGB survey instructions**. Do not hardcode a standards version; check the current official publisher.
   - https://clss.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en/surveystandards-normesdarpentage
   - https://natural-resources.canada.ca/maps-tools-publications/maps/boundaries-land-surveys/publications-canada-lands-surveys
3. **IOGC-specific guidance (only after confirming IOGC jurisdiction):**
   - Survey Plan Requirements: https://www.iogc-pgic.gc.ca/eng/1472575186371/1472575275357
   - Surface Contracts: https://iogc-pgic.gc.ca/eng/1100110010404/1100110010405
   - IOGC MyCLSS Checklist: https://www.myclss.ca/myclss/index/iogc-pgic/
   - Environmental requirements and updates: https://iogc-pgic.gc.ca/eng/1100110010401/1100110010402
4. **MyCLSS survey submission/plan checks and First Nation approvals:**
   - SGB plan check: https://www.myclss.ca/myclss/index/pc/
   - First Nation plan approvals: https://www.myclss.ca/myclss/index/fna/
   - NRCan: survey on land managed under a Land Code: https://natural-resources.canada.ca/maps-tools-publications/maps/boundaries-land-surveys/surveying-first-nation-lands-managed-under-land-code
5. **Individual interest evidence:** recorded CLSR plans, older provincial LTO plans where relevant, active land/contract/registry evidence, First Nation land-management records and actual project instructions. Recorded survey geometry and current instrument status are separate questions.
   - NRCan tools, Survey Plan Search and CLSS Map Browser: https://natural-resources.canada.ca/maps-tools-publications/maps/boundaries-land-surveys/tools-applications-canada-lands-surveys
6. **Forestry and environmental approvals:** use the current governing First Nation, IOGC and applicable federal/provincial requirements; do not infer timber-cutting permission from a survey or surface agreement. For background on varying reserve forestry regimes:
   - https://natural-resources.canada.ca/forests-forestry/sustainable-forest-management/legality-sustainability

## Verified distinguishing statements (recheck for current applicability)
- IOGC's *Survey Plan Requirements* distinguish **Surface Permit sketch plans** prepared to applicable provincial standards from formal application/CLSR survey plans; the webpage's older pre-2019 webinar is expressly not the source of present-day application procedure.
- The same IOGC requirements identify an Application Survey Plan and a CLSR Survey Plan, a separate Incidental Activities table, IA as non-permanent lands, limits/labels of existing contracts and a road allowance jurisdiction check. These are **context-specific**, not universal requirements for every exhibit.
- IOGC's *Surface Contracts* page identifies Surface Permit (SP) as a possible route for short-term **off-lease remediation** or temporary workspace adjacent to a ROW, and differentiates amendments affecting the underlying contract boundary from other changes.
- MyCLSS's general SGB plan check includes appropriate CLSR and LTO recording blocks, applicable endorsements, boundary dimensions, limited rights, source plan numbers and a match against registry records. Applicability depends on document and instructions.
- Reclamation and surrender are not synonyms for amendment. A site closure process can involve remediation, reclamation, access/temporary permits and later surrender.

## Authority notes
- An NRCan cadastral map is a discovery aid, not definitive proof of legal interests or a replacement for a recorded plan.
- A plan may contain both CLSR and provincial LTO numbers. Distinguish document identifiers from separate agreement identifiers.
- Historical plan/operator labels do not establish current agreement holders.
- Guidance can be general or dated: record the actual source revision, publication date, applicability and any conflicting project instructions. Where uncertain, status is `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
- Source material is linked, not reproduced. Do not commit private plan copies, screenshots, extracted real-client geometry or internal procedures.
