# Boundary edge decision protocol
For each candidate edge answer: which feature, which side, which two authoritative endpoints, which observation supports it, is it a subject course or tie/control, and what contradicts it? Assign stable SIDE_A/SIDE_B until direction is established.
A boundary may continue through an intersecting existing right-of-way; an intersection alone does not create a terminal. Existing boundaries can serve as control or legal terminals when independently supported. Physical tracks and trails are not automatically legal access boundaries.
Never bridge disconnected evidence for aesthetics or closure. If no supported edge can be established, preserve partial geometry and issue REVIEW_REQUIRED.
