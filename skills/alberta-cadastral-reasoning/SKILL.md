# Alberta Surveying & Cadastral Reasoning
Interpret ATS township, range, meridian, section and quarter-section descriptions with verified source fabric. Distinguish cadastral legal boundaries from travelled roads, physical fences and GIS approximations. Respect registered plan bearings, distances, monument descriptions and legal intent. Ordinary road allowances may be nominally 20.12 m, but use controlling plan evidence and check correction-line exceptions rather than assuming uniform width. Keep coordinate systems, ground/grid treatment, and alignment transformations explicit. Do not infer legal title, ownership, disposition status or survey authority from geometry alone. Verify current Alberta legislation, standards and authoritative records for any compliance decision.
