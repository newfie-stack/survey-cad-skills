# Alberta cadastral context
ATS uses meridian, range, township, section and quarter-section identifiers. Resolve the actual geometry from an authoritative ATS dataset or survey record rather than assuming an idealized square.
Road allowances are legal interests distinct from travelled roads. A nominal ordinary allowance of 20.12 m may be relevant, but correction-line roads and documented exceptions require independent boundary evidence. Record the applicable plan or statute and revision before relying on a width.
Distinguish NAD83 datum variants, UTM zones, grid bearings, ground distances and combined factors. Preserve source CRS and transformation parameters. Do not derive ownership, registration status or legal priority solely from spatial overlay.
