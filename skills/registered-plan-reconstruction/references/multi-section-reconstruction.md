# Multi-section reconstruction

Use when the subject crosses cadastral divisions or the plan prints separate areas by section, quarter or parcel. A corridor spanning multiple quarters is not necessarily multiple registered plans; keep plan identity, legal acquisition geometry and cadastral area partitions distinct.

1. **Inventory the scope.** Register each printed area and its section/quarter/parcel, unit, source locator and inclusion/exclusion notes. Distinguish witness/reference ties, verified cadastral limits and context-only labels or strokes. Do not make up control merely to attach the corridor to section corners.
2. **Build the legal footprint first.** In the local plan frame, construct the controlling side from observations, companion side from supported width and both terminals from evidence. Complete applicable closed acquisition components without forcing open control chains to close. Across an excluded road, use each component's own accepted control evidence; keep full pin-to-pin ties intact and subtract no nominal road width from a printed course.
3. **Partition only at established limits.** Resolve the legal split line from evidenced endpoints or verified control in the same frame. Intersect it with the accepted footprint using deterministic geometry, not image pixels or an approximate ATS overlay. Treat the split as an area boundary for reporting, not necessarily a new surveyed R/W edge. Keep the parent footprint, partition IDs and control/tie layer separate. Do not create an authoritative cut from a context-only line or extrapolate a short observed line as a legal limit without supporting evidence.
4. **Verify individual areas and the total.** For each non-overlapping piece, record its ID, exact legal scope, computed area, units and evidence. Sum all disjoint pieces belonging to each printed scope (including pieces divided by an excluded road); compare that sum once with the one printed area for that scope. Record the constituent piece IDs, printed area, total computed area, difference and status. Check that pieces cover the supported footprint without gap, overlap or double counting, and exclude any evidenced road gap. A matching aggregate cannot cancel opposite per-quarter errors. If a required split is unsupported, mark it NOT_EVALUATED and keep the affected result REVIEW_REQUIRED. Never tune geometry to fit a printed area.
5. **Separate local results from placement.** Record local geometry, cadastral area checks, absolute placement and reopened CAD verification as separate checks. A useful local DXF can be generated and reopened even when a required area or placement is unresolved. It is not a release in production coordinates until placement is independently verified. An area mismatch blocks full reconstruction acceptance even if the local footprint is useful. In a multi-plan assembly, register dependencies, favour the strongest independent evidence (usually work out from the oldest accepted controlling plan), and do not use provisional placement as authoritative control.
