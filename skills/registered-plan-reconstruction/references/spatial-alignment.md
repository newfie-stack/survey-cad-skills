# Multi-plan spatial integration
Reconstruct each plan independently in its own local mathematical frame before georeferencing. Identify published controls, accepted monuments, ATS points, shared monuments and reliable ties. Prefer the earliest plan with defensible control when later plans depend on its registered boundaries; chronology is a guide, not a substitute for evidence. Store a dependency graph showing each accepted anchor.
For a 2D similarity transformation, solve rotation, uniform scale and translation from accepted control. Use redundant controls and weighted least squares where justified; record residuals and reject mismatches. A two-point fit does not independently establish handedness: use a third non-collinear check or printed bearing/orientation evidence. Preserve local geometry separately from transformed output.
Keep authoritative newly surveyed coordinates fixed. Do not rubber-sheet a correctly reconstructed legal boundary to disguise a contradictory observation. Treat an approximate ATS or GIS overlay as a search aid until verified.
