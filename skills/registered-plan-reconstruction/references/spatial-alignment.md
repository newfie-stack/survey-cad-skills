# Multi-plan spatial integration
Reconstruct each plan independently in its own local mathematical frame before georeferencing. Identify published controls, accepted monuments, ATS points, shared monuments and reliable ties. Prefer the earliest plan with defensible control when later plans depend on its registered boundaries; chronology is a guide, not a substitute for evidence. Store a dependency graph showing each accepted anchor.
For a 2D similarity transformation, solve rotation, uniform scale and translation from accepted control. Identify trusted fitting anchors and independent check points before fitting. Use weighted least squares only among justified fitting anchors when warranted; keep independent checks out of the fit, report their residuals, and do not average away conflicting monument evidence. A two-point fit does not independently establish handedness: use a third non-collinear check or printed bearing/orientation evidence. Preserve local geometry separately from transformed output.
Keep authoritative newly surveyed coordinates fixed. Do not rubber-sheet a correctly reconstructed legal boundary to disguise a contradictory observation. Treat an approximate ATS or GIS overlay as a search aid until verified.

Report local reconstruction and spatial placement independently. If a supported local solution lacks a verified section/control association, keep its local coordinates and report ALIGNMENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED; an approximate ATS fit is review-only. An accepted direct survey anchor can support placement without using ATS. A failed or unverified parent placement cannot supply absolute control to another plan. A documented shared monument may support relative assembly without establishing absolute coordinates. Keep each plan's registration, evidence and local frame independently traceable.
