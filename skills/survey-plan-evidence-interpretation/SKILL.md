# Survey Plan Evidence Interpretation
Transcribe printed bearings, distances, widths, curves, offsets and monument notes exactly. Capture source page and detail. Interpret lineweight and symbols only alongside labels, endpoint identities and plan context. Distinguish found, placed, calculated and unmonumented evidence. Record boundary, tie, traverse, control and contextual linework separately. Where a printed full distance contains a partial segment, derive a remainder only with proven common alignment and endpoints. Do not infer legal boundaries or coordinates from visual proximity. Return an evidence table with original text, normalized value, source, candidate role, confidence, conflicts and REVIEW_REQUIRED items.
