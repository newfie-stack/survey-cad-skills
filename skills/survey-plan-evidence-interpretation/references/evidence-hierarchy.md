# Survey evidence interpretation
Classify found, placed, calculated, non-monumented and unknown points independently from their geometric roles. A monument symbol, annotation and line style must be interpreted in the context of the plan legend, field notes and surrounding observations. Keep raw OCR/manual transcription alongside normalized bearings and distances. Do not equate heavy strokes automatically with legal boundary or dashed strokes automatically with tie: check endpoints, labels and source detail.
Record conflicting interpretations explicitly. Distinguish plan annotation, direct measurement, computed remainder, and inferred relationship. Use source page/region for every material assertion. Prefer authoritative surveyed observations over image-fitting and GIS approximations; escalate contradictions instead of blending them.
